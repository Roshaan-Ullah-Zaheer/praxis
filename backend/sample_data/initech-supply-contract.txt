SUPPLY CONTRACT
Between Northwind Trading Co. ("Buyer") and Initech Manufacturing Ltd. ("Seller")
Effective Date: 22 February 2025

1. SUPPLY OBLIGATIONS
Seller agrees to manufacture and deliver precision parts in accordance with Buyer's
purchase orders.

2. PRICE AND PAYMENT
2.1 Prices are fixed for the first contract year.
2.2 Buyer shall pay each invoice within sixty (60) days of receipt (Net 60).
2.3 Early payment within ten (10) days earns a 2% discount.

3. TERM AND EXIT
3.1 This Contract runs for three (3) years from the Effective Date.
3.2 Either party may terminate for convenience with ninety (90) days' advance written
    notice.
3.3 Either party may terminate for insolvency of the other party immediately.

4. WARRANTY PERIOD
4.1 Seller warrants all parts against defects in material and workmanship for
    twenty-four (24) months following delivery.
4.2 Warranty claims must be submitted in writing with supporting documentation.

5. LIABILITY
5.1 Except for liability arising from intellectual property infringement, which is
    unlimited, each party's liability is capped at the value of the affected order.
5.2 Consequential damages are excluded.

6. CONFIDENTIALITY
The confidentiality obligations of this Contract survive for three (3) years after
termination.

7. GOVERNING LAW
This Contract is governed by the laws of England and Wales.
