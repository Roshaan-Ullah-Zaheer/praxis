NORTHWIND TRADING CO. — EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK (EXCERPT)
Human Resources — Revised: 1 March 2025

1. WORKING HOURS
Standard working hours are 9:00 to 17:30, Monday through Friday, with a one-hour lunch
break.

2. REMOTE WORK
2.1 Eligible employees may work remotely up to three (3) days per week.
2.2 Remote work arrangements must be approved by the employee's manager and reviewed
    quarterly.

3. PAID TIME OFF
3.1 Full-time employees accrue twenty (20) days of paid annual leave per year.
3.2 Up to five (5) unused days may be carried into the following year.
3.3 Public holidays are granted in addition to annual leave.

4. SICK LEAVE
Employees are entitled to ten (10) paid sick days per year. A medical certificate is
required for absences exceeding three (3) consecutive days.

5. CODE OF CONDUCT
Employees are expected to act professionally, respect colleagues, and avoid conflicts
of interest.

6. EXPENSES
Business expenses must be submitted within thirty (30) days with valid receipts and are
reimbursed in the next payroll cycle.

7. CONFIDENTIALITY
Employees must protect company and customer information both during and after their
employment.

8. PERFORMANCE REVIEWS
Formal performance reviews are conducted twice per year.
