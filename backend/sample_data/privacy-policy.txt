NORTHWIND TRADING CO. — PRIVACY POLICY
Last updated: 1 April 2025

1. WHO WE ARE
Northwind Trading Co. respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use,
and retain personal information about customers and website visitors.

2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT
We collect contact details, order history, payment metadata, and website usage data
through cookies and analytics.

3. HOW WE USE INFORMATION
We use personal information to fulfill orders, provide support, comply with legal
obligations, and improve our services.

4. DATA RETENTION
4.1 We retain customer personal data for up to one (1) year after your last
    interaction with us, after which it is deleted or anonymized.
4.2 Aggregated, anonymized statistics may be kept longer for analytics.

5. SHARING
We share personal data with service providers under contract and where required by
law. We do not sell personal data.

6. YOUR RIGHTS
You may request access, correction, deletion, or a copy of your personal data by
contacting privacy@northwind.example.

7. SECURITY
We protect personal data with industry-standard safeguards, including encryption and
access controls.

8. CONTACT
Questions about this policy can be sent to privacy@northwind.example.
