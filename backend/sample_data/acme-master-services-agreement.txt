MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT
Between Northwind Trading Co. ("Client") and Acme Industrial Supply LLC ("Vendor")
Effective Date: 14 January 2025

1. SCOPE OF SERVICES
Vendor shall provide industrial components and related fulfillment services as
described in each Statement of Work executed under this Agreement.

2. PAYMENT TERMS
2.1 Vendor shall invoice Client monthly for services rendered.
2.2 All undisputed invoices are due and payable within thirty (30) days of the
    invoice date (Net 30).
2.3 Late payments accrue interest at 1.0% per month.

3. TERM AND TERMINATION
3.1 This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for two (2) years.
3.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon thirty (30) days
    prior written notice to the other party.
3.3 Either party may terminate immediately for material breach not cured within
    fifteen (15) days of written notice.

4. WARRANTY
4.1 Vendor warrants that all delivered goods will be free from defects in materials
    and workmanship for a period of twelve (12) months from the date of delivery.
4.2 Client's exclusive remedy for breach of warranty is repair or replacement.

5. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
5.1 Each party's total aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the
    total fees paid by Client to Vendor in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim.
5.2 Neither party is liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages.

6. CONFIDENTIALITY
Each party shall protect the other's confidential information using no less than a
reasonable standard of care for a period of three (3) years following disclosure.

7. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.
