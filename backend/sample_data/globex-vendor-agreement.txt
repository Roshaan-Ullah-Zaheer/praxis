VENDOR SERVICES AGREEMENT
Between Northwind Trading Co. ("Client") and Globex Components Inc. ("Supplier")
Effective Date: 3 March 2025

1. ENGAGEMENT
Supplier will supply electronic components and logistics services per the attached
schedules.

2. INVOICING AND PAYMENT
2.1 Supplier issues invoices upon shipment of each order.
2.2 Payment is due within forty-five (45) days of the invoice date (Net 45).
2.3 Amounts not paid when due bear interest at 1.5% per month.

3. DURATION; TERMINATION
3.1 The initial term is eighteen (18) months and renews automatically for successive
    one-year periods unless terminated.
3.2 Either party may terminate for convenience by providing sixty (60) days' written
    notice prior to the intended termination date.
3.3 Either party may terminate for cause upon thirty (30) days' uncured material breach.

4. PRODUCT WARRANTY
4.1 Supplier warrants delivered products against defects for ninety (90) days from
    delivery.
4.2 Defective items will be replaced at Supplier's expense.

5. LIABILITY CAP
5.1 Supplier's maximum aggregate liability for any and all claims shall not exceed
    fifty thousand U.S. dollars ($50,000).
5.2 In no event shall either party be liable for lost profits or consequential loss.

6. CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION
Confidential information shall be held in confidence for five (5) years from the date
of disclosure.

7. JURISDICTION
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of New York.
