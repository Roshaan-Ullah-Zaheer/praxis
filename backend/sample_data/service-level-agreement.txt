SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT (SLA)
Attachment to the Vendor Services Agreement with Globex Components Inc.
Effective Date: 3 March 2025

1. SERVICE AVAILABILITY
1.1 Supplier's ordering portal shall be available 99.9% of the time, measured monthly,
    excluding scheduled maintenance.
1.2 Scheduled maintenance is limited to eight (8) hours per month, announced 48 hours
    in advance.

2. SUPPORT RESPONSE TIMES
2.1 Critical (Severity 1) issues: response within four (4) hours, 24x7.
2.2 High (Severity 2) issues: response within one (1) business day.
2.3 Standard (Severity 3) issues: response within three (3) business days.

3. SERVICE CREDITS
3.1 If monthly availability falls below 99.9%, Client is entitled to a 5% service
    credit on that month's fees.
3.2 If availability falls below 99.0%, the credit increases to 15%.
3.3 Service credits are Client's sole remedy for availability failures.

4. MEASUREMENT AND REPORTING
Supplier shall provide a monthly availability report within five (5) business days of
month end.

5. ESCALATION
Unresolved Severity 1 issues are escalated to the Supplier's duty manager after two (2)
hours.

6. EXCLUSIONS
Availability calculations exclude outages caused by Client systems, force majeure, or
third-party networks outside Supplier's control.
