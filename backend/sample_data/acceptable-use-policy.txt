NORTHWIND TRADING CO. — ACCEPTABLE USE POLICY
Applies to all users of company systems and the customer portal
Effective: 1 March 2025

1. PURPOSE
This policy sets out acceptable use of Northwind's information systems, network, and
customer-facing portal.

2. ACCEPTABLE USE
Systems are provided for legitimate business purposes. Incidental personal use is
permitted provided it does not interfere with work or violate this policy.

3. PROHIBITED ACTIVITIES
3.1 Sharing account credentials or allowing unauthorized access.
3.2 Attempting to bypass security controls or access data without authorization.
3.3 Uploading malware or unlawful content.
3.4 Using company systems to harass others or infringe intellectual property.

4. SECURITY RESPONSIBILITIES
4.1 Users must choose strong passwords and enable multi-factor authentication where
    available.
4.2 Suspected security incidents must be reported to security@northwind.example
    immediately.

5. MONITORING
The company may monitor system use for security and compliance, consistent with
applicable law and the Privacy Policy.

6. DATA HANDLING
Users must handle customer and company data in accordance with the Privacy Policy and
Records Retention Policy.

7. ENFORCEMENT
Violations may result in suspension of access and disciplinary action.
