NORTHWIND TRADING CO. — RECORDS RETENTION POLICY
Internal Policy — Finance & Legal
Approved: 10 January 2025

1. PURPOSE
This policy defines how long company records are retained to meet legal, tax, and
operational requirements.

2. SCOPE
This policy applies to all employees and to all business records, whether physical or
electronic.

3. RETENTION SCHEDULE
3.1 Financial and accounting records (ledgers, invoices, tax filings) shall be retained
    indefinitely for audit and historical reference.
3.2 Contracts and legal records shall be retained for ten (10) years following
    expiration or termination.
3.3 Routine operational correspondence shall be retained for two (2) years.

4. PERSONAL DATA
Records containing personal data are also subject to the Privacy Policy and any
applicable Data Processing Addendum; where retention periods conflict, the shorter
period required by data protection law applies to personal data specifically.

5. DESTRUCTION
Records that have reached the end of their retention period shall be securely destroyed
unless subject to a legal hold.

6. LEGAL HOLDS
Upon notice of litigation or investigation, affected records must be preserved
regardless of the standard retention schedule.

7. RESPONSIBILITY
The Finance Director and General Counsel jointly own this policy and review it annually.
